opsystem backup

An internal backup tool of Morco Polo Empire Ltd, trading as Homes Smith. Not a public service.

opsystem backup keeps encrypted copies of our own business system's backups in our own Google Drive, so that a copy is always kept away from our server.

  • It is used only by us, for our own data. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody can sign up or sign in to it.
  • It uses Google's limited drive.file permission, which only covers the specific Drive files used with this tool. In practice it works only with the backup files it has created itself. It cannot see or open any other files in Drive.
  • Every file is encrypted on our own server before it is uploaded. The passwords needed to decrypt it stay under our control only. Google stores the files but cannot read their contents.
  • The Google sign-in credentials are kept only on our server, readable only by authorised server administrators, and used solely for this backup connection.
